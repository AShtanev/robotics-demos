A robot dog that guides visitors on its own

We are a Dubai-based team of robotics developers. We build the software that lets a Unitree Go2 robot dog work among visitors without an operator: it navigates the building by itself and talks to people. The videos below show it in service at the Russian State Library in Moscow, plus the humanoid stage work the same team delivers.

01

Robot guide at the Russian State Library

The Lenin Library is one of the largest libraries in the world. A Unitree Go2 running our software works there as an exhibition guide: it leads visitors along a route, stops at the exhibits and tells about them, answers questions, moves through the halls on a lidar map of the building, and walks back onto its charging dock when the tour is over. The project is running now, has been shown on television, and a second robot for the library is under discussion.

What this gives a venue

  • A guide visitors remember and film

    A walking robot dog draws attention to an exhibition in a way a screen or an audio guide does not.

  • Tours without extra staff

    The robot follows a set route with stops. Route and stories are changed in software when the exhibition changes.

  • Your languages

    The robot at the library speaks Russian. English, Arabic and other languages are added for a new venue.

  • No changes to the building

    No markers, rails or beacons. A one-time mapping walk is all the robot needs.

How a pilot would start

We start small, with one route in one hall or one exhibition, and run it for a trial period with our support on site.

  1. One mapping walk through the chosen hall.
  2. A route with stops and the stories for each stop, prepared together with your team, in your languages.
  3. A trial period with the robot working among visitors and our team supporting it.
02

Robot conductor

A humanoid on the conductor's podium for Rosconcert's show Symphony of Innovations. The robot is synchronised with the control booth: on its cue a video sequence starts and the stage lighting changes.

03

Robot dancer

A humanoid performing a duet with choreographer Alexander Mogilev at the All-Russian Competition of Ballet Dancers and Choreographers in Yoshkar-Ola. The choreography is captured from the dancer, retargeted to the robot and performed live.
